Why the costs sit where they do

You said both partners work together on the leads and split 50/50, so every dollar in and every dollar out belongs to the partnership — regardless of whose bank account it moved through. Whose card was charged is custody, not ownership. So:

  • Tyson's $799.31 campaign cost is a partnership cost — he is reimbursed for the half that isn't his.
  • Berry's $30 TFN is a partnership cost on exactly the same footing.
  • The $152 in Tyson's account and the $48 in Berry's are partnership revenue they happen to be holding.

Settle-up formula, same for both: your half of the net, minus what you collected, plus what you paid.
